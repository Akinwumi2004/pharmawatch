## 2. Evaluation Limitations

Testing uses simulated batch and verification records. The resulting scores and classifications demonstrate prototype behaviour and do not establish real-world counterfeit-detection accuracy. Further validation against appropriately labelled real-world data would be required.

## 3. Evaluation Summary

The initial functional tests confirm that batch retrieval, risk assessment, and dashboard prioritisation operate as expected. Automated and scenario-based testing will provide additional evidence of the model's behaviour.
